Life with Fam

July 2026

The only person who truly gets me is my older cousin. She’s turning 18 while I’m just 15, but when we’re together, the age difference disappears and it feels like we’re the same. We understand each other in a way no one else does—not even our parents. We share everything, from our thoughts to our secrets, and somehow we always end up liking and hating the same things. Everything about us just matches, like we were meant to find ourselves in each other.

These are some of our moments together.
